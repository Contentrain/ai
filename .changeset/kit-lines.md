Band gains `headingLines` and `leadLines`, Testimonial gains `quoteLines`, by Hero's `headingLines` rule: with `lines`, each line the author broke the text into (`\n`) is a block of its own, `data-kit-line="1"`, `"2"`, …, for a theme or a design layer to set its ink, indent or size. A heading stays one heading and a lead or a quote one paragraph; a quote's marks go before its first line and after its last. The default, `flow`, prints the text exactly as before.
