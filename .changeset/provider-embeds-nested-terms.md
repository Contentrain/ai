Embed frames a Google Form at its embed address (`docs.google.com/forms/…/viewform`) and the hand-pasted map iframe `google.com/maps?q=…&output=embed`; the classic mapping places a known provider's iframe (`classic/provider-embed`: Calendly, Typeform, Google Forms, Spotify, SoundCloud, Loom, Wistia, OpenStreetMap) as an Embed instead of leaving it behind. The astro starter fills a custom taxonomy's `:path` from the term's parent chain, so a nested term keeps its WordPress address (`/project-type/infrastructure/bridges/`), as a category does.
