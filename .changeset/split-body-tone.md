Split's body follows its band: on an inverse, accent or brand band (or a measured dark fill) the markdown body is drawn in the band's own text colour and muted role, as its heading is. Before, it kept the page's ink, so on an inverse band it was invisible (1:1) and on a fill it was under 4.5:1. A link in the body on a fill is drawn in the fill's ink. On the page's own surface nothing changes. The kit visual now fails a body text under 4.5:1 (3:1 large) on its band.
