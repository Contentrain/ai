**ContactForm: new `align` axis (`center` | `start`).** `center`, the default, is unchanged: the stacked column, and the details alone before a Studio form, sit centred at a reading width. `start` gives them the section's whole column from its start edge. This is how a page builder's container lays out text that sets no alignment of its own (IP-4 Elementor contact: G9 at 1280 went from .585 to .897).
